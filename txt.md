this is an app for managing family members from one grand-parent: Nzombokwe. we will then list his children, then his grand-children, and so on. individuals are there as members. we will be able to know the genealogy of each member.
- secondary, the app manages contribution that are given by members of the genealogy, members who give contribution have a particular status and the contribution is in two categories:
- social and auto-develpment. a member can contribute on both types and each contribution type is counted separately for are to be used differentlyas their names
- when we know a member, we can see his status(about contribution)
- we have a treasury side, where we record entrees and sorties each month with libelee to say a desc of what made an in/out.
- treasury is sanctioned by a montly report, a journal and a summary of each type o contribution
- on the social side, we give support to members in need (mariage, illness, death,..) and this will be recorded as sortie in the social journal
- on the auto-dev side, we can buy a land, a tool, ... and this will be marked too
